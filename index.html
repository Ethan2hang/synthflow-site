Three moves. No menus.

Every stage is real: the planner maps your words to synth parameters, the engine renders an actual WAV, and each edit is stored as its own immutable version.

01
Describe

Type what you hear in your head, or drop a reference file. Keywords are pulled out so you can see how the sentence was read.

02
Listen

A rendered preview, not a mock-up. Play it, scrub it, download the WAV, and read the parameters that produced it.

03
Refine

Say “brighter” or “shorter attack”. Each change is a new snapshot; undo, redo and full history are always one click away.

Drop a WAV you like. SynthFlow’s audio model estimates the filter and envelope settings behind it and hands you a preset you can keep editing in words.
